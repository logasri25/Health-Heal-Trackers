Ear Health

1. Otitis Media (Middle Ear Infection)

An infection or inflammation of the middle ear, commonly caused by bacteria or viruses.

Symptoms:

  • Ear pain
  • Difficulty hearing
  • Fluid discharge from the ear
  • Fever
  • Feeling of fullness in the ear

Precautions:

  • Maintain proper ear hygiene
  • Avoid exposure to cigarette smoke
  • Get timely medical treatment
  • Keep ears dry when advised by a doctor

2. Tinnitus (Ringing in the Ears)

A condition in which a person hears ringing, buzzing, or other sounds without an external source.

Symptoms:

  • Ringing in the ears
  • Buzzing or humming sounds
  • Difficulty concentrating
  • Sleep disturbances
  • Hearing difficulties

Precautions:

  • Avoid loud noises
  • Use ear protection in noisy environments
  • Keep headphone volume low
  • Consult an ENT specialist

3. Hearing Loss

A condition in which a person has difficulty hearing sounds partially or completely.

Symptoms:

  • Difficulty hearing conversations
  • Frequently asking others to repeat words
  • Increasing television volume
  • Difficulty hearing in noisy places

Precautions:

  • Avoid prolonged exposure to loud sounds
  • Use hearing protection
  • Get regular hearing tests
  • Seek medical advice for hearing difficulties

4. Otitis Externa (Swimmer's Ear)

An infection or inflammation of the outer ear canal, often caused by trapped moisture.

Symptoms:

  • Ear pain
  • Itching inside the ear
  • Redness and swelling
  • Fluid discharge
  • Pain when touching the ear

Precautions:

  • Keep ears dry after swimming
  • Avoid inserting objects into the ears
  • Do not scratch the ear canal
  • Consult a doctor if symptoms occur

5. Ménière's Disease

An inner ear disorder that affects balance and hearing, often causing episodes of vertigo.

Symptoms:

  • Repeated episodes of dizziness
  • Ringing in the ears
  • Hearing loss
  • Feeling of fullness in the ear
  • Nausea and vomiting

Precautions:

  • Follow medical advice
  • Maintain regular sleep habits
  • Manage stress
  • Discuss dietary changes with a doctor

6. Earwax Impaction

A condition in which excessive earwax builds up and blocks the ear canal.

Symptoms:

  • Blocked feeling in the ear
  • Reduced hearing
  • Earache
  • Ringing in the ears
  • Itching or discomfort

Precautions:

  • Avoid using cotton buds inside the ear
  • Do not insert sharp objects into the ear
  • Seek professional earwax removal if needed
  • Use ear drops only with appropriate medical advice

7. Otosclerosis

A condition involving abnormal bone growth in the middle ear, which can cause hearing loss.

Symptoms:

  • Gradual hearing loss
  • Difficulty hearing low-pitched sounds
  • Ringing in the ears
  • Balance problems in some cases

Precautions:

  • Get regular hearing examinations
  • Consult an ENT specialist
  • Use prescribed hearing aids if needed
  • Follow recommended treatment

8. Labyrinthitis

Inflammation of the inner ear structures responsible for hearing and balance, often associated with infection.

Symptoms:

  • Severe dizziness
  • Loss of balance
  • Nausea and vomiting
  • Hearing loss
  • Ringing in the ears

Precautions:

  • Take adequate rest
  • Avoid driving during dizziness
  • Stay hydrated
  • Seek medical attention for symptoms

9. Acoustic Neuroma

A rare, usually non-cancerous tumour that develops on the nerve connecting the inner ear to the brain.

Symptoms:

  • Gradual hearing loss, usually in one ear
  • Ringing in one ear
  • Balance difficulties
  • Dizziness
  • Facial numbness in some cases

Precautions:

  • Seek medical advice for unexplained hearing loss
  • Get recommended hearing tests
  • Follow specialist monitoring
  • Attend regular medical checkups

10. Perforated Eardrum

A condition in which a hole or tear develops in the eardrum due to infection, injury, or loud noise.

Symptoms:

  • Sudden ear pain
  • Hearing loss
  • Fluid or blood discharge
  • Ringing in the ears
  • Dizziness

Precautions:

  • Keep the affected ear dry
  • Avoid inserting objects into the ear
  • Do not use ear drops without medical advice
  • Consult an ENT specialist
