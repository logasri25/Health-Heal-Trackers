Eye Health

1.Myopia (Nearsightedness)

A condition where nearby objects appear clear, but distant objects are blurry

Symptoms:

  • Blurred distant vision
  • Squinting
  • eye strain
  • headaches

Precautions

  • Regular eye checkups
  • wear prescription glasses or contact lenses
  • avoid excessive screen time

2.Hyperopia (Farsightedness)

A condition where distant objects are clearer than close ones

Symptoms:

  • Blurry close-up vision
  • eye strain
  • headaches
  • difficulty reading

Precautions:

  • Use corrective lenses
  • take breaks from reading
  • maintain a balanced diet for eye health

3.Astigmatism

A condition caused by an irregularly shaped cornea or lens, leading to blurred vision

Symptoms:

  • Blurred or distorted vision
  • eye strain
  • headaches
  • difficulty seeing at night

Precautions:

  • Use prescribed glasses or contact lenses
  • get regular eye exams

4.Presbyopia

Age-related loss of near vision due to reduced lens flexibility

Symptoms:

  • Difficulty reading small print
  • eye strain
  • need to hold objects farther away to see clearly

Precautions

  • Use reading glasses
  • avoid straining the eyes
  • ensure good lighting

5.Conjunctivitis (Pink Eye)

Inflammation of the conjunctiva, usually due to infection or allergies

Symptoms:

  • Redness
  • itching
  • watery eyes
  • discharge
  • swollen eyelids

Precautions:

  • Maintain hygiene
  • avoid touching eyes
  • wash hands regularly
  • use prescribed eye drops

6.Keratitis

Inflammation of the cornea, often due to infection or injury

Symptoms:

  • Eye redness
  • eye pain
  • blurred vision
  • sensitivity to light
  • excessive tearing

Precautions:

  • Avoid contact lens overuse
  • maintain eye hygiene
  • seek medical help for eye infections

7.Uveitis

Inflammation of the uvea (middle layer of the eye)

Symptoms:

  • Eye pain
  • eye redness
  • blurred vision
  • light sensitivity
  • floaters

Precautions:

  • Protect eyes from infections
  • wear sunglasses
  • treat underlying conditions

8.Blepharitis

Inflammation of the eyelids due to bacterial infection or skin conditions

Symptoms:

  • Red
  • swollen eyelids
  • crusty eyelashes
  • itching
  • burning sensation

Precautions:

  • Maintain eyelid hygiene
  • use warm compresses
  • avoid eye makeup

9.Cataracts

Clouding of the eye’s lens, leading to blurry vision

Symptoms:

  • Blurry vision
  • sensitivity to light, faded colors
  • trouble seeing at night

Precautions:

  • Wear sunglasses
  • eat a diet rich in antioxidants
  • avoid smoking

10.Ectopia Lentis

Displacement of the eye’s lens, often due to genetic disorders or trauma

Symptoms:

  • Blurred vision
  • double vision
  • light sensitivity

Precautions:

  • Regular eye checkups
  • avoid eye injuries
  • seek medical advice for genetic conditions
