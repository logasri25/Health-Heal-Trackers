Stomach Health

1.Gastritis

Inflammation of the stomach lining, often caused by infection, alcohol, stress, or medications like NSAIDs.

Symptoms:

  • Upper abdominal pain or discomfort
  • Nausea and vomiting
  • Bloating
  • Loss of appetite
  • Indigestion
  • Black stools (in severe cases, indicating bleeding)

Precautions

  • Black stools (in severe cases, indicating bleeding)
  • Limit NSAIDs and painkillers.
  • Eat smaller, frequent meals.
  • Manage stress
  • o Take prescribed medications (e.g., proton pump inhibitors, antibiotics for H. pylori infection)

2.Pectic Ulcer Disease(PUD)

Sores that develop on the lining of the stomach or the first part of the small intestine, usually due to an infection with Helicobacter pylori or the long-term use of NSAIDs.

Symptoms:

  • Burning stomach pain (often between meals or at night)
  • Nausea
  • Bloating
  • Loss of appetite
  • Weight loss
  • Vomiting blood or passing black stools (indicating bleeding)

Precautions:

  • Avoid alcohol, spicy foods, and smoking.
  • Limit NSAID use
  • Take prescribed medications such as proton pump inhibitors and antibiotics
  • Manage stress and eat smaller,frequent meals/li>

3.Gastroesophageal Reflux Disease (GERD)

A condition where stomach acid or bile irritates the food pipe lining, leading to heartburn and acid regurgitation.

Symptoms:

  • Heartburn (a burning sensation in the chest or throat)
  • Regurgitation of food or sour liquid
  • Difficulty swallowing
  • Chronic cough or sore throat
  • Chest pain
  • Hoarseness or lump in the throat

Precautions:

  • Avoid foods that trigger acid reflux (e.g., citrus, spicy foods, fatty foods)
  • Eat smaller, more frequent meals
  • Avoid lying down immediately after eating
  • Lose weight if overweight
  • Take medications like antacids or proton pump inhibitors (PPIs) as prescribed

4.Irritable Bowel Syndrome (IBS)

A gastrointestinal disorder that affects the large intestine, causing symptoms such as cramping, bloating, and diarrhea or constipation.

Symptoms:

  • Abdominal pain or cramping
  • Bloating and gas
  • Diarrhea or constipation
  • Mucus in the stool

Precautions

  • Eat a fiber-rich diet
  • Avoid trigger foods (e.g., dairy, caffeine, fatty foods)
  • Practice stress-reducing techniques like yoga or meditation
  • Drink plenty of fluids
  • Use medications to manage symptoms (e.g., laxatives, antispasmodics)

5.Gastroparesis

A condition where the stomach takes too long to empty its contents, often due to nerve damage, commonly caused by diabetes.

Symptoms:

  • Nausea and vomiting
  • Early satiety (feeling full after eating only a small amount)
  • Bloating
  • Abdominal pain
  • Weight loss

Precautions:

  • Eat smaller, more frequent meals
  • Avoid high-fat and high-fiber foods
  • Control blood sugar levels if diabetic
  • Medications to improve gastric motility (e.g., prokinetics)
  • Consider a low-residue diet to ease digestion

6.Stomach Cancer (Gastric Cancer)

: Cancer that develops in the stomach lining, often linked to infection with Helicobacter pylori, smoking, or a family history of gastric cancer.

Symptoms:

  • Abdominal pain or discomfort
  • Unexplained weight loss
  • Loss of appetite
  • Nausea and vomiting
  • Difficulty swallowing
  • Blood in stool or vomit

Precautions:

  • Regular screenings for high-risk individuals (e.g., those with a family history or H. pylori infection)
  • Avoid smoking and limit alcohol consumption
  • Eat a balanced diet rich in fruits and vegetables
  • Seek medical advice early if symptoms occur

7.Food Poisoning

Illness caused by consuming contaminated food or water, often caused by bacteria (like Salmonella or E. coli), viruses, or parasites

Symptoms:

  • Nausea and vomiting
  • Abdominal cramps
  • Diarrhea (often watery or bloody)
  • Fever

Precautions:

  • Practice good food hygiene (washing hands, cooking food thoroughly)
  • Avoid consuming undercooked or raw meats and eggs
  • Drink plenty of fluids to stay hydrated
  • Seek medical attention if symptoms are severe or persistent

8.Celiac Disease

An autoimmune disorder where the ingestion of gluten (a protein found in wheat, barley, and rye) causes damage to the small intestine lining

Symptoms:

  • Diarrhea or constipation
  • Abdominal bloating or pain
  • Fatigue
  • Unexplained weight loss
  • Skin rashes (dermatitis herpetiformis)

Precautions:

  • Strictly avoid foods containing gluten
  • Read food labels carefully
  • Follow a gluten-free diet to manage symptoms and prevent long-term damage
  • o Take nutritional supplements if needed to address deficiencies

9.Helicobacter Pylori Infection

An infection caused by the bacterium Helicobacter pylori, which can lead to gastritis, peptic ulcers, and in some cases, stomach cancer

Symptoms:

  • Abdominal pain
  • Nausea
  • Loss of appetite
  • Bloating
  • Frequent burping

Precautions:

  • Treatment with a combination of antibiotics and proton pump inhibitors (PPIs)
  • Avoid alcohol, smoking, and spicy foods that irritate the stomach lining
  • Practice good hygiene, including washing hands and drinking clean water

10.Lactose Intolerance

A condition where the body is unable to properly digest lactose, a sugar found in milk and dairy products, due to a deficiency in lactase enzyme

Symptoms:

  • Bloating
  • Diarrhea
  • Gas
  • Stomach cramps

Precautions:

  • Avoid dairy products or consume lactose-free alternatives
  • Use lactase supplements to help digest lactose
  • Include calcium-rich foods (or supplements) in the diet to make up for the lack of dairy intake
