Heart Health

1. Coronary Artery Disease (CAD)

A condition where the coronary arteries become narrowed or blocked due to plaque buildup (atherosclerosis), restricting blood flow to the heart muscle.

Symptoms:

  • Chest pain
  • Shortness of breath
  • Fatigue
  • Palpitations:

Precautions

  • Maintain a healthy diet (low in saturated fats and cholesterol).
  • Exercise regularly and maintain a healthy weight.
  • Avoid smoking and excessive alcohol consumption
  • Regular check-ups for cholesterol and blood pressure.

2. Heart Attack (Myocardial Infarction)

A medical emergency that occurs when blood flow to a part of the heart is blocked, leading to the death of heart tissue.

Symptoms:

  • Chest pain or discomfort
  • Shortness of breath
  • Nausea or vomiting
  • Lightheadedness or dizziness

Precautions:

  • Eat a heart-healthy diet.
  • Engage in regular physical activity.
  • Control risk factors such as high blood pressure, high cholesterol, and diabetes.
  • Avoid smoking.

3. Heart Failure

A condition where the heart is unable to pump blood effectively, leading to inadequate blood flow to meet the body's needs.

Symptoms:

  • Shortness of breath, especially while lying flat
  • Swelling in the legs, ankles, or abdomen
  • Fatigue
  • Rapid or irregular heartbeat

Precautions:

  • Maintain a low-sodium diet.
  • Take prescribed medications as directed (e.g., diuretics, ACE inhibitors).
  • Monitor weight regularly (for fluid retention).
  • Limit alcohol and avoid smoking.

4. Arrhythmia

An irregular heartbeat, where the heart may beat too quickly, too slowly, or erratically.

Symptoms:

  • Palpitations (rapid or irregular heartbeat)
  • Dizziness or lightheadedness
  • Shortness of breath
  • Chest pain

Precautions

  • Avoid stimulants like caffeine and alcohol.
  • Follow prescribed medications or treatments (e.g., antiarrhythmic drugs, pacemakers).
  • Manage stress and maintain a healthy lifestyle.

5. Valvular Heart Disease

A condition where one or more of the heart’s valves don’t function properly, affecting blood flow through the heart.

Symptoms:

  • Fatigue
  • Shortness of breath, especially during physical activity
  • Swollen ankles or feet
  • Heart murmur (detected during a physical exam)

Precautions:

  • Monitor for signs of worsening symptoms..
  • In some cases, surgery or valve replacement may be necessary.
  • Regular check-ups and monitoring by a cardiologist.

6. Cardiomyopathy

A disease of the heart muscle that makes it harder for the heart to pump blood effectively, leading to heart failure.

Symptoms:

  • Fatigue
  • Shortness of breath
  • Swelling in the legs, ankles, or abdomen
  • Irregular heartbeat

Precautions:

  • Take prescribed medications as directed.
  • Limit salt intake and maintain a low-sodium diet.
  • Regular medical check-ups and monitoring of heart function.

7. Hypertension (High Blood Pressure)

A condition where the force of the blood against the walls of the arteries is consistently too high, which can damage the heart and other organs over time.

Symptoms:

  • Often no noticeable symptoms (hence it is called the "silent killer").
  • Severe cases can cause headaches, dizziness, or shortness of breath.

Precautions:

  • Monitor blood pressure regularly.
  • Maintain a healthy diet (low salt, low fat).
  • Exercise regularly and avoid excessive alcohol consumption.
  • Take prescribed medications as directed.

8. Congenital Heart Disease

A heart defect present at birth, which can involve structural problems with the heart's walls, valves, or blood vessels.

Symptoms:

  • Fatigue
  • Shortness of breath
  • Poor feeding in infants
  • Cyanosis (bluish skin color, especially around lips or fingers)

Precautions:

  • Early diagnosis and management are crucial.
  • Surgery may be required in severe cases.
  • Regular follow-up with a cardiologist.

9. Endocarditis

An infection of the inner lining of the heart chambers and valves, typically caused by bacteria.

Symptoms:

  • Fatigue
  • Shortness of breath.
  • Fever
  • Swelling in the legs or abdomen

Precautions:

  • Practice good dental hygiene to avoid bacterial infection.
  • Use prophylactic antibiotics before surgeries or dental procedures if at high risk.
  • Seek immediate medical attention for signs of infection.

10. Pericarditis

Inflammation of the pericardium (the sac surrounding the heart), which can result from infection or injury.

Symptoms:

  • Fatigue
  • Shortness of breath
  • Sharp, stabbing chest pain (worse when breathing deeply or lying down)
  • Swelling in the abdomen or legs

Precautions:

  • Treatment with anti-inflammatory medications.
  • Rest and avoid excessive physical exertion during recovery.
  • In severe cases, fluid drainage from the pericardial sac may be required
