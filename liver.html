Liver Health

1.Hepatitis (A, B, C, D, E)

Inflammation of the liver, commonly caused by viral infections (Hepatitis A, B, C, D, and E). The types are transmitted through different routes such as contaminated food, blood, and sexual contact.

Symptoms:

  • Fatigue
  • Jaundice (yellowing of the skin or eyes)
  • Nausea and vomiting
  • Abdominal pain (especially in the upper right side)
  • Dark urine
  • Loss of appetite

Precautions

  • Hepatitis A and E: Practice good hygiene (handwashing) and avoid contaminated food/water
  • Hepatitis B and C: Avoid sharing needles, unprotected sex, and ensure proper sterilization of medical instruments
  • Vaccination is available for Hepatitis A and B
  • For Hepatitis C, antiviral treatments can help manage the disease

2.Cirrhosis

Chronic liver damage leading to scarring of the liver tissue, which can impair liver function. The most common causes are chronic alcohol use, viral hepatitis, and fatty liver disease

Symptoms:

  • Jaundice
  • Swelling in the legs or abdomen (ascites)
  • Fatigue
  • Itchy skin
  • Loss of appetite and weight loss
  • Nausea
  • Confusion or memory issues (hepatic encephalopathy)

Precautions:

  • Avoid alcohol consumption
  • Regular medical check-ups and monitoring of liver function
  • Treat underlying causes such as viral hepatitis or fatty liver
  • Medications to manage complications like fluid retention or encephalopathy

3.Fatty Liver Disease (Non-Alcoholic and Alcoholic)

The accumulation of excess fat in liver cells, which can lead to inflammation and liver damage. Alcoholic fatty liver disease is caused by excessive alcohol consumption, while non-alcoholic fatty liver disease (NAFLD) is linked to obesity, diabetes, and high cholesterol

Symptoms:

  • Fatigue
  • Abdominal discomfort (especially in the upper right side)
  • Jaundice (in severe cases)
  • No symptoms in the early stages

Precautions:

  • Lose weight if overweight or obese
  • Avoid excessive alcohol consumption
  • Manage diabetes and cholesterol levels
  • Eat a balanced diet and exercise regularly

4.Liver Cancer (Hepatocellular Carcinoma)

A primary cancer of the liver that often arises in people with chronic liver disease, such as cirrhosis or hepatitis

Symptoms:

  • Unexplained weight loss
  • Abdominal pain or fullness
  • Jaundice
  • Nausea and vomiting
  • Loss of appetite
  • Swelling in the abdomen (due to fluid accumulation)

Precautions

  • Vaccination for Hepatitis B to reduce the risk of liver cancer
  • Regular liver screenings (ultrasound or blood tests) for those at high risk (chronic hepatitis or cirrhosis)
  • Avoid alcohol and maintain a healthy weight
  • Seek treatment for any underlying liver conditions

5.Liver Fibrosis

Early scarring of the liver tissue due to chronic liver damage, often caused by hepatitis, alcohol use, or fatty liver disease.

Symptoms:

  • Often no symptoms in the early stages
  • Fatigue
  • Jaundice (in advanced stages)
  • Swelling in the abdomen or legs

Precautions:

  • Treat underlying conditions (e.g., hepatitis or fatty liver)
  • Avoid alcohol and manage weight
  • Regular check-ups with liver function tests
  • If advanced, consider treatments like antivirals or liver transplant

6.Liver Abscess

A collection of pus within the liver, often caused by bacterial infections, parasites, or trauma

Symptoms:

  • Fever
  • Abdominal pain (especially in the upper right side)
  • Nausea and vomiting
  • Fatigue
  • Jaundice (in severe cases)

Precautions:

  • Early diagnosis and treatment with antibiotics
  • Drainage of abscess if necessary
  • Preventive measures include proper hygiene and avoiding contaminated food or water

7.Hemochromatosis

A genetic disorder in which the body absorbs too much iron, which can accumulate in the liver and other organs, leading to organ damage

Symptoms:

  • Fatigue
  • Joint pain
  • Abdominal pain
  • Unexplained weight loss
  • Darkening of the skin

    • Precautions:

    • Regular blood donations (phlebotomy) to remove excess iron
    • Avoid iron-rich foods and vitamin C supplements
    • Lifelong monitoring of iron levels and liver function

8.Wilson's Disease

A genetic disorder that leads to the buildup of copper in the liver and other organs, causing liver damage and neurological symptoms

Symptoms:

  • Fatigue
  • Abdominal pain
  • Jaundice
  • Tremors or muscle stiffness
  • Personality changes or psychiatric symptoms

Precautions:

  • Lifelong treatment with medications to remove excess copper (e.g., chelating agents)
  • Avoid copper-rich foods
  • Regular monitoring of copper levels in the body

9.Autoimmune Hepatitis

A condition in which the body's immune system attacks the liver, leading to inflammation and liver damage

Symptoms:

  • Fatigue
  • Jaundice
  • Abdominal discomfort (especially in the upper right side)
  • Dark urine
  • Nausea and vomiting

Precautions:

  • Immunosuppressive medications (e.g., corticosteroids) to control inflammation
  • Regular follow-up with a hepatologist
  • Avoid alcohol and other liver toxins

10.Liver Failure (Acute and Chronic)

A condition in which the liver suddenly (acute) or gradually (chronic) loses its ability to function. It can result from hepatitis, cirrhosis, liver cancer, or drug toxicity

Symptoms:

  • Jaundice
  • Confusion or disorientation (hepatic encephalopathy)
  • Swelling in the abdomen or legs
  • Nausea and vomiting
  • Fatigue

Precautions:

  • Early treatment of underlying liver disease
  • Avoid alcohol and liver-toxic drugs
  • In severe cases, liver transplantation may be necessary
